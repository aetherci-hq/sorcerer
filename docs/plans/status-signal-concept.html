Status Signal

Status communicated through shape and motion — not color memorization. One color family. Distinct silhouettes. Animation means alive, stillness means not. The shape tells you what kind of alive.

Design Principles
Shape as language
Each state has a distinct silhouette. You never need to decode a color — the form tells you.
Motion as vitality
If it moves, it's alive. The type of motion tells you what it's doing. No motion means at rest.
Monochromatic
One warm neutral tone at varying intensities. No traffic light palette. No color memorization required.
Peripheral legibility
Scannable in peripheral vision. Active items catch the eye through motion; everything else recedes.
The Five States
Active
Radiating ping. Broadcasting. Alive.
Idle
Flat dash. At rest. Session ended.
Waiting
Spinning arc. Processing. Thinking.
Starting
Breathing dot. Coming alive.
Archived
Hollow ring. Faint. Historical.
